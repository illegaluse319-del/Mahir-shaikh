About the Initiative

As part of the ESG theme 2026–27, the Department of Commerce & Management Science launched the "Waste to Worth" drive under the banner of 'Cleaner and Greener Tomorrow'.


Students creatively repurposed post-consumer plastic waste into functional trash cans, eco-structures, and educational branding artwork, reinforcing that responsible citizenship begins with small, deliberate actions.

"This student-led initiative turning waste into value shall inspire many to make responsible choices in life."

— Dr. Mazahar Ahmed Farooqui, Principal

The efforts were recognized and appreciated in the presence of Principal Dr. Mazahar Ahmed Farooqui, IQAC Coordinator Dr. J. D. Shaikh, Head of Department, and esteemed faculty members. The college continues to lead as a recognized Green Champion in institutional sustainability.
